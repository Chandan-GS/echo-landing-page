The daily briefing

Your whole day, in four calm minutes.

No scrolling. No dozens of notifications to triage. Set a time — 7 AM, say — and Echo delivers one spoken briefing that ties together what actually happened: the meetings, the messages that mattered, the reminders you'd have missed.

  • Wake up already caught up — before you've unlocked your phone.
  • A live countdown shows exactly when your next briefing lands.
  • Regenerate any time to fold in what just came in.
Two brains, your choice

On-device by default. Cloud when you want speed.

Echo can think entirely on your phone with a compact local model — or, if you prefer, hand off to Google Gemini for faster results. You decide, and you can switch any time.

Recommended · fully private

On-device engine

A quantized Qwen2.5 1.5B model runs locally. No internet, no server, no cost — your data never leaves the phone.

  • Works completely offline
  • Nothing transmitted, ever
  • Free — no subscription
Optional · faster

Google Gemini engine

Prefer snappier, richer responses? Opt into the cloud engine and Echo sends the relevant text to Gemini to generate your briefing.

  • Faster generation
  • Off by default — you opt in
  • Switch back anytime
Ask Echo

Ask a question. Get a real answer.

Everything Echo has captured is searchable in plain language. Type or speak — “What's on my schedule?”, “Any messages from work I missed?” — and Echo answers from your own history, showing exactly which notifications it drew from.

  • Chat or full-screen voice mode — hands-free while you get ready.
  • Every answer cites its sources so you can trust it.
  • Grounded in your data — no made-up details.
Immersive voice

A conversation, not a command line.

Tap into voice mode and Echo becomes a full-screen, hands-free companion. Watch the sound sprite ripple as it listens, thinks and speaks — and see the exact notifications behind every reply, right there on screen.

  • Speak naturally and interrupt any time.
  • A living mascot reflects every state — listening, thinking, speaking.
  • Expand the sources drawer to see what Echo used.

Privacy first — not as an afterthought

Your day is nobody's business but yours.

Echo reads sensitive things — your messages, your calendar, your notifications. So we built it to keep them where they belong: on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you deliberately turn on the optional cloud engine.

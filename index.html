Installing Echo
1

Download & Play Protect

When you download the file, Android may show a 'Play Protect' warning. Tap 'More details' and then 'Install anyway' OR Go to play store -> profile -> play protect -> settings -> turn off.

2

Enable Restricted Settings

After installation, open the app. If your phone blocks the Notification Access permission, go to Settings > Apps > Echo, tap the three-dot menu (⋮) in the top right, and select 'Allow restricted settings'.

3

Complete Setup

Return to the app to enable the final permissions.
